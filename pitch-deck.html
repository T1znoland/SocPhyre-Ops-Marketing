The Solution

Think like an attacker.
Defend like one.

"Don't tell me what your scanner found. Show me how an actual attacker would get in, move laterally, and exfiltrate data — then fix the path, not just the vulnerability."
— The SOCPHYRE approach

Every engagement starts from the adversary's perspective:

What's the fastest way in? What's the weakest link — technology, process, or human? Once inside, how far can we go? What's the blast radius?

Then we close those paths. Not with generic recommendations, but with specific, tested mitigations informed by real attack methodology.
